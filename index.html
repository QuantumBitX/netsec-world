Addresses, names and identifiers are illustrative examples. The handshake shown is EAP-TLS with TLS 1.2 (RFC 5216); EAP-TLS 1.3 (RFC 9190) shortens the flight but keeps the same roles. World 1-2 shows WPA2-Enterprise with CCMP-128; WPA3-Enterprise uses the same steps and makes Protected Management Frames mandatory. World 1-3 shows RadSec (RADIUS over TLS, RFC 6614, being revised by the IETF RADEXT group) and the Blast-RADIUS attack (CVE-2024-3596) it prevents.
